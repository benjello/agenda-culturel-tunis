Tomorrow at Habibi Downtown Radio.

A live session in two movements.

First, Hedi Fahem on guitar and Hsin Benmiloud on nay and kawala come together for an ambient journey, blending strings, breath and textures into an intimate, immersive soundscape.

In the second movement, HABIIIBIJI joins them, bringing electronic layers, hypnotic rhythms and deeper grooves into the mix.

From ambient explorations to a more groove-driven live experience, let the sounds unfold.
