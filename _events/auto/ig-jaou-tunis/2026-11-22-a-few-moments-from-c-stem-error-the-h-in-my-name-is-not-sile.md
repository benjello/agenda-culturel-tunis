A few moments from C!STEM ERROR: The “H” in My Name Is Not Silent by Nadim Bahsoun, presented at Le 4ème Art on 24 October.

Through movement, music and storytelling, Nadim brought his grandmother’s memory to the stage, exploring displacement, exile, queerness and the strength it takes to survive.

A performance about the stories we carry, and the memories that live on through generations.

Jaou Tunis ’26
Contemporary Art Biennale
