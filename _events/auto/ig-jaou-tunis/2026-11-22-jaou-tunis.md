Jaou Tunis
Live Performance
LABES
Selim Ben Safia

What does it mean to stand still, and what does it take to keep moving?

Created by choreographer Selim Ben Safia, LABES brings together five performers to explore vulnerability, resilience and the strength to resist stillness, with a scenography by Nadia Kaabi-Linke.
