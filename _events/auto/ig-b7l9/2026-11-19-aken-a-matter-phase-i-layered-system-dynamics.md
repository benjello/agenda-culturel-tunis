AKEN — A:/MATTER — PHASE I: LAYERED SYSTEM DYNAMICS

Join us at B7L9 Art Centre for the release party and immersive listening session of Aken’s debut album.

Recorded in his bedroom in La Marsa, the album brings four machines into conversation, exploring rhythms, loops and shifting patterns. Rather than composing melodies, Aken discovers them through improvisation, allowing the machines to shape the music as it unfolds.
