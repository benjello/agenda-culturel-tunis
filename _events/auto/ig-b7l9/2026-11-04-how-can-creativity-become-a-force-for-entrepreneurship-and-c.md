How can creativity become a force for entrepreneurship and change?

Join us at B7L9 Art Centre on Wednesday, November 4, from 7:00 PM to 8:30 PM for Creativity, Entrepreneurship & Change, an interactive talk and workshop led by Dr. Meg Peterson from King's College London.

What happens when a creative idea grows beyond its creator? When people, relationships and resources come together to turn it into something that creates value and drives change?
