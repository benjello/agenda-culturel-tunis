A big thank you to everyone who came to visit 'Kids of the World, Unite!', an exhibition celebrating 10 years of The Funambulist Magazine.

Our special thanks go to Shivangi Mariam Raj for presenting the exhibition and sharing insights into the magazine's work and archive, and to artists Mouna Karray and Nidhal Chamekh for accepting our invitation to take part in this exhibition and bring their work into dialogue with The Funambulist.
