Designed as an invitation to participate, this exhibition seeks to think equally with our children as well as our elders, contemplating the material conditions of the production and circulation of knowledge. It invites us to envision the collective consciousness we can develop together by expanding our conception of the classroom.

The exhibition opens at 32bis, Rue Ben Gedhahem, Tunis (third floor) on November 6, 2026, at 5:00 PM and will be on view till January 15, 2027.
