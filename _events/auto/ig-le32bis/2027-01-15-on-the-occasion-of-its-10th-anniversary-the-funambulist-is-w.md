On the occasion of its 10th anniversary, The Funambulist is welcomed by 32Bis from November 6, 2026, to January 15, 2027, to reflect on collective forms, methods, and strategies for building our liberation pedagogies.

Thinking equally with our children as well as our elders, this intervention focuses on shared learning.
